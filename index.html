01 THE MACHINE + METHOD

The Megaformer.
Built for tension.

The Megaformer combines a moving carriage, stable platforms, handlebars, cables and straps with adjustable spring-loaded resistance.

As you push, pull and stabilize, the springs create continuous resistance through the working range. Paired with Lagree’s slow tempo, the resistance keeps your muscles under tension longer.

WHY SLOW WORKS

Slow, controlled movement keeps your muscles working under tension. This challenges slow-twitch endurance and deep-core control—including pelvic-floor coordination—while progressively loading the muscles and connective tissues that support your joints.
